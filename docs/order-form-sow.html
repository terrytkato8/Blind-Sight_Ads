Part C — Statement of Work

Studio deliverables, for each Placement

  1. A concept sketch or mockup showing the Placement in the Game's art style.
  2. A 3D model, textures and materials (and audio sting where applicable), built to Schedule C.
  3. Integration through the sponsor-slot system on the listed maps, with gameplay parity confirmed by automated test.
  4. Approval submissions: rendered screenshots plus a short in-engine capture.
  5. Go-Live in a public build, with a confirmation email and screenshots.
  6. Reports as set out in Schedule B.

Sponsor deliverables

  1. Brand assets per Schedule C within 10 business days of signing.
  2. Brand guidelines and any mandatory legal lines.
  3. One named approver who responds within 5 business days.
  4. Payments when due.
