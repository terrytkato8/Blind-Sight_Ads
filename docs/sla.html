Agreements

Service Level Agreement (Schedule B)

Schedule B to the Master Sponsorship Agreement. Measurable commitments for each party, and the credits Sponsor receives if Studio misses them. "BD" means business days, Pacific Time.

1. Studio commitments

Area Commitment Measured by
Placement availability The Placement appears and renders correctly in at least 99% of eligible matches on its listed maps, each calendar month Sponsor-slot telemetry
Delivery Milestones met as set in the Order Form's SOW, counted from receipt of complete Sponsor assets Order Form dates vs. actual
Revisions Revised submission within 5 BD of Sponsor feedback (10 BD for interactive props) Email timestamps
Go-Live confirmation Screenshots and confirmation within 2 BD of Go-Live Email
Launch report Within 30 days of Go-Live Report delivered
Quarterly report Within 15 BD of each quarter end Report delivered
Term-end removal Removed or replaced in a public build within 30 days of the Term ending (not Permanent Placements) Patch notes and build
Emergency disable Disabled via the remote kill switch within 24 hours of a valid morals, legal or Severity 1 request Kill-switch log
Contact response Reply within 2 BD Email timestamps

2. Defect severity and response

Severity Example Acknowledge Mitigate Fix
1 — Brand harm Wrong or outdated logo, off-brand or offensive state, missing legal line 1 BD Kill switch within 24 hours Next patch, at most 10 BD
2 — Visible defect Missing in some matches, broken texture, audio sting not playing 2 BD — Within 20 BD
3 — Cosmetic Minor lighting or clipping issues 5 BD — Next scheduled update

3. Sponsor commitments

  • Complete brand assets per Schedule C within 10 BD of signing the Order Form.
  • Approval or specific written changes within 5 BD of each submission; otherwise deemed approved (MSA 6.2).
  • One named approver, plus a backup.
  • Payment on time. Studio's SLA timers pause while payments are more than 30 days overdue.

4. Reporting contents

Per Placement:

  • Impressions. Counted when the Placement covers at least 10% of the screen, unobstructed, for at least 1 continuous second.
  • Interactions. Pickups, throws and activations, where applicable.
  • Reach. Unique players exposed, match count and average exposure time.
  • Creator and stream reach. Streams and clips featuring the Game, as reported by participating creators.
  • Availability. Percentage for the period, plus any incidents.

All data is aggregated and anonymous. No personal data is shared.

5. Service credits

Monthly availability Credit
95% to under 99% 5% of that month's pro-rated sponsorship fee
90% to under 95% 15%
Under 90% 30%

Late delivery caused by Studio: if Go-Live slips more than 10 BD past the SOW date because of Studio, the Term is extended by the length of the delay.

How credits work: applied as a Term extension or credit toward a future Placement, at Sponsor's choice, capped at 30% of the period's pro-rated fee. Credits are Sponsor's sole remedy for SLA misses, unless a miss is also a material breach under MSA 12.2.

6. Exclusions

The SLA doesn't apply to delays or outages caused by:

  • Steam or other platform outages, or the player's own hardware or network
  • scheduled maintenance announced 48 hours in advance
  • Sponsor's late assets, approvals or payments
  • changes required by platform policy or law (MSA 13)
  • force majeure
