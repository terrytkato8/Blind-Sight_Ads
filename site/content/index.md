# Blind Sight — Brand Partnerships Hub

The home for **in-game product placement and sponsorship in Blind Sight**, a Kato.8 Studios LLC game. Pricing, plans, term sheets, agreements and operating documents all live here.

> **Draft status.** Every legal document on this site is a working draft for review by a licensed California attorney before use. Finance and revenue-recognition guidance needs a CPA's confirmation. Bracketed fields like [Brand], [date] and [$__] are filled in per deal.

## The program in one paragraph

Blind Sight is a 4–8 player asymmetric horror-comedy for PC (Steam). A nearly blind Hunter tracks everyone else by sound alone, while Hiders sneak, throw decoys and sell each other out. Brands buy **in-game product placements**: branded throwables, interactive props, set dressing, video screens, posters and a title sponsor slot. Pricing is a flat fee for a fixed term, plus a small one-time implementation fee. Branded items never give a gameplay advantage.

## Order of use

1. **Before any outreach:** collect signed Contributor IP Assignments from the whole team. Get Creator LOIs from committed streamers.
2. **Outreach:** send the RFP and the sponsor term sheet, and the brand returns its brief.
3. **Close:** sign the Master Sponsorship Agreement (once per brand) and an Order Form for each placement. The SLA and Creative Guidelines are attached.
4. **Deliver:** engineering follows the Tech Spec, and finance invoices and tracks the deal in the contract tracker.
