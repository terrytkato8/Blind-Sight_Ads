# Sponsorship RFP & Brand Brief

Part 1 is the RFP Kato.8 sends to prospective sponsors. Part 2 is the brief the brand fills in and returns. Send both with the pitch deck and the rate card.

## Part 1 — Request for Proposal

**Issued by:** Kato.8 Studios LLC · **Contact:** Terry Teng, Founder & CEO · **Issued:** [date] · **Responses due:** [date]

**1. The opportunity.** Blind Sight is a 4–8 player asymmetric horror-comedy for PC (Steam). One nearly blind Hunter tracks everyone else by sound alone, while Hiders sneak, throw decoys and sell each other out. Kato.8 Studios is offering a limited number of founding brand partners a place inside the game before launch.

**2. What we're offering.** Branded throwables, interactive props (vending machines, jukeboxes, speakers), set-dressing props, in-world video screens, posters and a title sponsor slot. Pricing is in the [rate card](rate-card.html). Founding partners who sign before launch get 30% off and can buy permanent placements.

**3. What we're asking for.** Please return the Brand Brief (Part 2) with:

- the placements and categories you're interested in
- your preferred term and whether you need exclusivity
- budget range and decision timeline
- any brand restrictions or mandatory legal lines

**4. Timeline**

**5. How we pick partners.** Category fit with the game's audience; comfort with comedic, physical treatment of the product; budget and term; ability to co-promote; timing.

**6. Available on request.** A gameplay capture of a sample branded placement *(in production)*, creator partner details, the brand-safety one-pager and the asset spec (Schedule C).

**7. Terms.** This RFP is not an offer and creates no obligation. Proposals and discussions are confidential. Kato.8 Studios may accept or decline any response. Categories are allocated on a first-signed basis.
