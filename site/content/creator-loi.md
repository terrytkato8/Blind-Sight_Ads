# Creator Letter of Intent

A short, mostly non-binding letter that documents a streamer's intent to play and promote Blind Sight. The deck and the RFP rely on these, so collect one from every creator we name.

---

**Kato.8 Studios LLC** · Hollywood, California · [date]

Dear [Creator name / channel],

Thank you for your interest in **Blind Sight**, our upcoming asymmetric multiplayer horror-comedy for PC. This letter records what we've discussed.

**1. Your intent.** You intend to:

- play Blind Sight on stream or in published videos within [30] days of its public release (target: [launch window]);
- produce at least [__] stream(s) or video(s) and [__] short-form clip(s) featuring the game;
- let Kato.8 Studios name you as a launch creator partner in sponsor and investor-facing materials.
