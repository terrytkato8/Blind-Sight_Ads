# Master Sponsorship Agreement

*Draft for attorney review. Not legal advice.*

This Master Sponsorship Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of [Effective Date] between **Kato.8 Studios LLC**, a California limited liability company ("Studio"), and **[Sponsor legal name]**, a [state/entity type] ("Sponsor").

## 1. Definitions

- **Game:** Blind Sight, developed and published by Studio, including updates, patches and marketing materials.
- **Placement:** a branded in-game item, surface or screen described in an Order Form.
- **Order Form:** a signed document that describes specific Placements, fees, term and deliverables.
- **Sponsor Marks:** Sponsor's trademarks, logos, product designs, trade dress and audio identifiers supplied under this Agreement.
- **Go-Live:** the date a Placement first appears in a publicly available build of the Game.
- **Term of a Placement:** the period in its Order Form, starting on the later of the Game's public release and Go-Live.

## 2. Structure

2.1 Each Placement is governed by this Agreement and its Order Form. If they conflict, the Order Form controls for that Placement only.

2.2 Schedule B ([Service Level Agreement](sla.html)) and Schedule C ([Brand Asset & Creative Guidelines](creative-guidelines.html)) are part of this Agreement.

## 3. Studio obligations

3.1 Studio will design, build and integrate each Placement as described in the Order Form, adapting Sponsor Marks to the Game's art direction.

3.2 Studio will meet the service levels in Schedule B.

3.3 Studio will provide reports as described in Schedule B, using aggregated, anonymous data only.

## 4. Sponsor obligations

4.1 Sponsor will deliver brand assets that meet Schedule C within [10] business days of signing the Order Form.

4.2 Sponsor will review submissions within the approval windows in Section 6.

4.3 Sponsor will pay all fees when due.

## 5. Gameplay integrity

5.1 Studio has sole control over the Game's design, gameplay, balance, tone, maps and content.

5.2 Every branded item has gameplay attributes identical to its unbranded equivalent, including physics, noise radius, damage, speed, cooldowns and availability. No Placement gives any player a gameplay advantage.

5.3 Players will not be required to watch or interact with any Placement to play, and will not be rewarded for engaging with a Placement.

5.4 Sponsor accepts that the Game is a stylized horror-comedy. Branded items may be thrown, dropped, dented, knocked over and used as decoys, and may appear in scenes with a stylized weapon. Placements will never appear on the Hunter's weapon, on elimination effects, or in a disparaging context.

## 6. Approvals

6.1 Sponsor's approval rights cover only how Sponsor Marks are depicted: accuracy, color, logo use and brand guidelines.

6.2 Sponsor will approve or give written, specific change requests within **5 business days** of each submission. If Sponsor doesn't respond in time, the submission is deemed approved.

6.3 Two approval rounds per Placement are included. Further rounds are billed at $100 per hour and may move delivery dates.

## 7. Licenses

7.1 **Sponsor to Studio.** Sponsor grants Studio a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license to use, reproduce, adapt and display Sponsor Marks in the Game, and in marketing for the Game (trailers, screenshots, store pages, social media, press, events and streams), during the Term of each Placement. For a Placement designated **Permanent**, the license is perpetual and irrevocable for that Placement.

7.2 **Studio to Sponsor.** Studio grants Sponsor a non-exclusive license during the Term to use the Game's name, logo and approved footage to announce and promote the partnership, subject to Studio's prior written approval of each use.

7.3 No other rights are granted. Neither party may use the other's marks in a way that implies endorsement beyond the partnership.

## 8. Content that persists after the Term

When a Term ends, Studio will remove or replace the Placement in future builds as described in Schedule B. Studio is not required to recall, edit or take down (a) builds already distributed to players, (b) gameplay footage, streams, clips, screenshots, reviews or press created during the Term by Studio or third parties, or (c) archived marketing materials published during the Term.

## 9. Ownership

Sponsor owns Sponsor Marks. Studio owns the Game and all 3D models, textures, audio and code it creates, excluding Sponsor Marks. Neither party may use the branded models outside the Game without a separate written agreement.

## 10. Fees and payment

10.1 Fees and payment dates are set in each Order Form. The implementation fee is non-refundable once production starts.

10.2 Invoices are due Net 30. Late amounts bear interest at 1% per month or the maximum lawful rate, whichever is lower. Studio may pause work on any Placement with fees more than 30 days overdue.

10.3 Fees exclude taxes. Sponsor pays any applicable sales, use or similar taxes, other than taxes on Studio's income.

## 11. Release timing

11.1 Studio will use reasonable efforts to release the Game within its announced launch window, but release dates are not guaranteed.

11.2 If the Game is not publicly released by [Outside Date], Sponsor may terminate any Order Form for that Placement and receive a refund of all sponsorship fees paid for it. Implementation fees are not refunded.

## 12. Term and termination

12.1 This Agreement continues until every Order Form has expired or been terminated.

12.2 Either party may terminate an Order Form if the other materially breaches and doesn't cure within 30 days of written notice.

12.3 **Morals clause.** Either party may terminate an Order Form on written notice if the other party, or its principals, become the subject of public conduct or controversy that would reasonably harm the terminating party's reputation.

12.4 **Effect of termination.** Studio disables the Placement within the times in Schedule B.

- If Sponsor terminates for Studio's breach, or for Studio's conduct under 12.3, Studio refunds the unearned sponsorship fee pro rata.
- If Studio terminates for Sponsor's breach, or for Sponsor's conduct under 12.3, no refund is due and unpaid fees for the Term become payable.

## 13. Platform compliance

Studio may change, move or remove a Placement where needed to comply with platform policies (including Steam's advertising rules), law or a rating board requirement. That is not a breach. Studio will offer an equivalent Placement or a pro-rata refund.

## 14. Representations and warranties

14.1 Each party has authority to enter this Agreement.

14.2 Sponsor warrants that it owns or controls Sponsor Marks and that they don't infringe third-party rights, and that its products and any claims it supplies are lawful.

14.3 Studio warrants that the Game, excluding Sponsor Marks, is original or properly licensed, and that Studio holds written IP assignments from its contributors.

## 15. Indemnities

Each party will defend and indemnify the other against third-party claims arising from (a) its breach of its warranties, (b) its marks or materials infringing third-party rights, or (c) its own products or conduct.

## 16. Limitation of liability

Neither party is liable for indirect, incidental, special or consequential damages. Each party's total liability under an Order Form is capped at the fees paid or payable under that Order Form in the 12 months before the claim. These limits don't apply to indemnity obligations, breaches of confidentiality, or willful misconduct.

## 17. Confidentiality

Neither party may disclose the other's confidential information, including deal terms and unreleased Game content, except to advisors bound by confidentiality or as required by law.

## 18. Advertising law and disclosure

Each party complies with advertising laws that apply to its own activities, including FTC endorsement and disclosure rules. Sponsor won't ask creators or Studio to make product claims it hasn't substantiated.

## 19. Insurance

Each party maintains commercially reasonable general liability insurance. Studio maintains media liability (errors and omissions) insurance once [threshold] is reached.

## 20. General

20.1 **Assignment.** Neither party may assign this Agreement without consent, except to a successor or acquirer of all or substantially all of the business or assets it relates to.

20.2 **Force majeure.** Neither party is liable for delays caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including platform outages.

20.3 **Governing law and disputes.** California law. The parties first try mediation in Los Angeles County; remaining disputes go to the state or federal courts in Los Angeles County.

20.4 **Other.** Entire agreement; changes in writing; notices to the addresses below; independent contractors; electronic signatures and counterparts allowed.
