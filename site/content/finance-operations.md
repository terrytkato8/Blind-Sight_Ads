# Finance Operations — Invoicing, Revenue & Contract Tracking

Set this up before the first invoice goes out. The [contract tracker spreadsheet](downloads.html) implements everything below. *Review the revenue recognition policy with a CPA.*

## 1. Invoicing procedure

1. **On signature:** log the deal in the tracker, assign Order Form no. BS-YYYY-###, and save the signed MSA and Order Form in the contracts folder.
2. **Invoice 1 (on signature):** implementation fee + 50% of the sponsorship fee. Net 30.
3. **Invoice 2 (on Go-Live):** the remaining 50%, sent the same day as the Go-Live confirmation.
4. **Performance bonus:** invoiced only once the metric is verified, within 10 BD of the 90-day post-launch mark.
5. **Extra approval rounds:** billed at $100/hr on the next invoice, with hours logged.
6. **Collections:** reminders at day 31 and day 45. Pause work at day 60 (MSA 10.2), then escalate to the CEO.
7. **Records:** collect a W-9 from US sponsors before the first invoice. Payments go to the Kato.8 Studios LLC business account only.

## 3. Revenue recognition policy (draft)

| Item | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Cash received before Go-Live | Recorded as **deferred revenue** (a liability), not income |
| Sponsorship fee (fixed term) | Recognized evenly over the Term, starting at the later of Go-Live and public release |
| Implementation fee | Usually not a separate deliverable from the placement, so deferred and recognized over the Term too. Confirm with the CPA |
| Permanent placement | No end date, so the CPA sets a recognition period (e.g. the game's expected commercial life) |
| Performance bonus | Recognized only once the metric is achieved |
| Service credits / refunds | Reduce revenue in the period granted |
| Agency commission | Recorded as a cost, separate from revenue, if we pay it |

*Tax filing may use a different method than book accounting (e.g. cash basis); the CPA decides.*

## 4. Revenue share and bonus

Book sponsorship revenue to its own income account ("Sponsorship — Blind Sight"), separate from game sales. Each quarter, export recognized sponsorship revenue from the tracker, calculate the bonus under the Revenue Share Agreement, and publish the calculation to contributors.

## 5. Contract tracker tabs

| Tab | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| Deals | One row per Order Form: sponsor, placement, map(s), category, exclusivity, term, dates, fees, status |
| Invoices | Each invoice with due date, paid date, days overdue and status |
| Revenue Schedule | Monthly recognized revenue per deal, from term dates |
| Category Map | Which categories are sold on which maps, so slots aren't double-sold |
| Contributors | IP assignment status for every contributor |
| Creators | LOI status and audience numbers for every creator |

## 6. Monthly close checklist

- [ ] All Go-Lives this month invoiced
- [ ] Overdue invoices chased
- [ ] Revenue Schedule rolled forward and the journal entry posted (deferred to recognized)
- [ ] Service credits from the SLA report applied
- [ ] Term ends in the next 60 days flagged for renewal outreach
- [ ] Contributor and creator tabs up to date
